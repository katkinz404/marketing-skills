# Day-0 Checklist

Use this list on the first working day, before anything is published, sent, or funded. Every item here starts a clock that nothing downstream can shorten, or is an identity gate that only you, the owner, can pass. The AI agent pre-fills every form field it can from the intake; you execute in the order below.

Budget about four focused hours (judgment). Do not skip ahead to drafting or paid channels until items 1 through 8 are done or logged as a dated missing decision.

## How to use this list

- Work top to bottom. The order is by clock length, longest first, so the slow reviews run while you finish the rest.
- **Owner-only** items cannot be delegated to a VA or an AI agent: they verify your identity, your address, or your consent.
- "Agent pre-fills" means the agent drafts the exact text or values into the private workspace before you sit down. You paste, check, and submit.
- Anything you cannot complete today becomes a row in the missing-decisions table with an owner and a due date. Blanks are "Unknown; do not publish."

## Item details

### 1. Intake

Complete every field of the intake template. Hard requirements: one legal or brand name with no service or city keywords, one answered phone number, one URL, one real base address you can film at (not a PO box, mail store, or coworking desk), services with inclusions and exclusions, real service towns, who answers and when, capacity in team-hours per week, pricing method, the five economics numbers, your state, trust facts exactly as truthfully statable, existing accounts and who owns each, network inventory, month-1 cash ceiling, and consent decisions.

### 2. EIN

Apply online at the IRS if you do not have one. Free, minutes (government, irs.gov; re-verify on the live page). It unlocks Low-Volume Standard carrier registration for texting instead of the sole-proprietor tier, and cleaner advertiser verification. Never let a VA or agency obtain it under their identity.

### 3. Google Business Profile setup choices

Create the profile under a Google account you control. Use these exact choices.

| Field | Choose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Business model question (early in the flow) | "I travel to customers" | Hides the address; a visible home address on a service-area profile invites suspension. Trade press, Feb 2026, medium confidence; re-verify on the live page. |
| Business name | Real-world name only, no service or town keywords | Google name policy, support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 (platform_primary, snippet-confirmed; re-verify on the live page) |
| Primary category | "House cleaning service" or the trade's equivalent (for example "Carpet cleaning service", "Window cleaning service", "Pressure washing service") | Primary category is the top single local ranking factor (Whitespark Local Search Ranking Factors 2026, industry_research, Nov 2025, snippet-confirmed) |
| Address | Enter the real base address for verification, then keep it hidden | Address must be cleared or hidden when customers are not served there, support.google.com/business/answer/9157481 (platform_primary, snippet-confirmed) |
| Service areas | Real towns only, up to 20, whole area within about 2 hours of driving | answer/9157481 (20 areas) and answer/3038177 (about 2 hours, soft guideline); platform_primary, snippet-confirmed; re-verify on the live page |
| Hours | The window a human or the receptionist actually answers | "Open at time of search" is a ranking input (industry_research, Whitespark 2026); missed calls in stated hours may be billable on LSA from Oct 1, 2026 (snippet-confirmed, unverified) |
| Phone and website | The one answered number and the one URL from the NAP block | Identical NAP on every listing |
| Services | Leave until verified; then tick only Google's predefined services you sell | Predefined services affect ranking, custom ones do not (Sterling Sky, industry_research) |

**If a verified profile already exists**, skip the video and the 14-day freeze does not apply. Instead: confirm the profile is not restricted, walk the table above field by field against the NAP block, and batch any corrections over several days rather than in one sitting. A dormant profile that changes many core fields at once can trigger re-verification (judgment). If the profile has no reviews, the review loop is the binding constraint from Day 1: queue the identical, un-incentivized ask to every completed job, and run `past-customer-review-outreach.md` for past customers you can legitimately contact (paced, personal, no incentive).

### 4. Verification video shot script (skip if already verified)

Google requires one continuous, unedited take of 30 seconds or more, recorded inside the verification flow on a phone, not uploaded (support.google.com/business/answer/14271705, platform_primary, snippet-confirmed; re-verify on the live page). Film at the base address. Never film at a client's home. Keep faces out of frame.

| Seconds | Show | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0-8 | Street sign or house number, then the building or storage location | Location context |
| 8-18 | Branded vehicle, uniform, or labeled supplies and equipment | Evidence of the business |
| 18-26 | A document showing the business name (registration, insurance certificate, utility bill) | Evidence of the business |
| 26-35+ | Unlock a door: garage, storage unit, or office | Proof of authorization |

Say the business name aloud while filming. If the app asks for more, keep rolling; stopping and restarting creates two takes and a rejection. Google states review takes up to 5 business days; industry guides report appeals of 1-6 weeks (Sterling Sky, Whitespark; industry_research). Log the submission screenshot.

### 5. Google Ads advertiser verification

Create the account, add billing, and start advertiser verification today even though no campaign runs for weeks. It is the slowest clock and an agency manager account does not bypass it (trade press, 2026, low confidence; re-verify on the live page). Sole proprietors verify with government ID; entities use organization documents. Grant a VA Standard access later; never share the login.

### 6. Thumbtack account

Open the pro account in your own name and complete identity verification and background-check consent (owner-only). Paste the agent's profile text and job preferences. Set the weekly budget to $0 and leave it there. Thumbtack sets each lead's price and shows it in product; the activation gate later compares that quoted price to your shared-marketplace Max CPL.

### 7. Field-service software trial

Start a 14-day trial (Jobber or Housecall Pro; vendor-reported; verify at signup) unless you already pay for a scheduler. Enable two-way texting inside the app so no self-registered texting number sits on the launch critical path. Submit the vendor's carrier registration form anyway; approval can take 10-15 business days (Twilio-authored guidance, vendor, 2026). Record the trial end date; the purchase decision is a Day-14 item.

### 8. Local Services Ads screening (conditional)

Submit only if a registered entity and a general-liability certificate exist. Screening covers you and every field worker and commonly takes 1-3+ weeks (trade press, 2026, low confidence). It is not a Day 0-14 lead source. If either prerequisite is missing, log a missing-decision row with a due date instead.

### 9. VA job post

Post on Upwork and OnlineJobs.ph the same evening. Frame the role as deliverables and an SLA, not hours, with a W-9 or W-8BEN required before the first payment. Attach a 20-minute paid screening task: log five sample leads into the sheet, draft one marketplace reply from the approved template, and flag the one lead outside the service area. Decide the data-minimization rule before posting: the VA sees first name, phone, ZIP, and service only. First productive hour is Day 5-7 at the earliest (judgment).

### 10. If you clean personally

Post the first cleaner job today. Post-to-solo is 21-28 days in a clean run, 30 for planning, 40-45 with one fallout (judgment). A solo operator absorbs about 4-6 new recurring clients before reaching 80% utilization (judgment), which is inside that window. Use Indeed (free plus a small sponsored budget), Craigslist, Homebase, and local groups, with a referral bonus if you choose one. Three knockout questions, a paid working interview, and a background check before any solo home entry. W-2 is the safe default; consult counsel licensed in your state before paying a cleaner as a contractor.

Also plan for the AI receptionist to take first ring during jobs; that configuration is a Day 1-3 item.

## The 14-day no-edit rule

From the moment the profile shows Verified, do not edit the name, address, phone, primary category, or hours for 14 days, and make no bulk edits. Complete the description, services, photos, and Q&A once, from the approved pack, on the day it verifies. Early edits to core fields are a common trigger for re-verification or suspension (industry_research, judgment on the 14-day figure).
