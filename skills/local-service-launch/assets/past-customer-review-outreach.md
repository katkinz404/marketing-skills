# Past-Customer Review Outreach

Use this when a business has a verified Google Business Profile with few or no reviews and a backlog of real past customers who are reachable only through the owner's personal social accounts (Facebook friends, Instagram followers, neighborhood groups). It turns a one-time "message everyone" impulse into a paced, compliant, trackable loop.

This is guidance, not legal advice. Consult counsel licensed in the business's state if any question of consent, endorsement, or data handling is unclear.

## Why not message everyone at once

- **Google filters velocity spikes.** A profile that averages zero reviews and then receives many in a few days gets extra scrutiny, and Google may remove the reviews, adjust the rating, or temporarily disable new reviews, even when every review is genuine (industry guidance and Google community reports, 2025-2026, vendor and trade tier; no threshold is published by Google). Spread asks over weeks so reviews arrive at a natural rate.
- **Messenger rate-limits personal accounts.** Sending many similar messages quickly, or messaging many people who are not friends, can trigger a temporary sending block that lasts from a day to several weeks (messenger.com/help/177066345680802, platform_primary, snippet-confirmed; re-verify on the live page). Vary wording, personalize, and space sends.
- **A single burst spends the whole asset in one week.** Paced asks give the profile a steady review cadence for two months, which is what a new searcher and a ranking system both read as a living business.

## Eligibility: who may be asked

Ask only people who meet every line. Anyone who fails one line is not asked.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| They actually paid for the service, personally or for their household | Google prohibits reviews that do not reflect a genuine experience (support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114, platform_primary, snippet-confirmed; re-verify on the live page) |
| They are not family, employees, contractors, or business partners | Undisclosed insider reviews violate FTC 16 CFR 465.5 (government, effective 2024-10-21) and Google's conflict-of-interest rule |
| The owner remembers a real detail about the job (the home, the period, what was done) | The personal detail is what makes the message honest and what keeps it from reading as a template |
| The experience can be described truthfully, including how long ago it was | A review of a job from years ago is permitted if the reviewer says so; a review that implies a recent job that did not happen is not |

Work the list from most recent and most enthusiastic to oldest. Recent customers write faster, more specific reviews.

## The rules that never bend

- **No incentive of any kind**: no discount, gift, entry, credit, or "next clean on me." Google bans all review incentives; FTC 16 CFR 465.4 bans incentives conditioned on sentiment. The safest configuration is none.
- **No gating**: never ask "were you happy?" first and only send the link to the happy ones. Same ask to everyone eligible.
- **The reviewer writes it.** Never draft their review, never suggest phrases or a star rating, never post on their behalf, never ask them to mention a specific person. If they ask what to write, the only answer is: "Two honest sentences about what you remember is perfect."
- **Google only.** Do not ask for Yelp reviews (Yelp prohibits solicitation). Facebook Recommendations on the business page can be a later, separate ask; one message asks for one action.
- **Personal account, one person at a time.** No group message, no Page broadcast, no automation, no copy-paste with the name swapped. The owner sends every message.
- **Review requests stay separate from offers.** No "and if you ever need a clean" in the same message. A rebooking or referral note, if any, is a separate message at least two weeks later.
- **Customer names and contact details live in the private workspace, never in this repository.**

## Pacing plan (judgment, 2026-09-14)

| Week | Sends | Expect | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5-6, the warmest recent customers | 2-4 reviews | Confirm the link works and the profile displays reviews before scaling |
| 2-6 | 4-5 per week, no more than 2 per day, spread across days | 2-3 reviews per week | Send nudges to non-responders from the prior week |
| 7-10 | Remaining names, same cadence | 1-2 per week | Stop when the list is worked; do not re-ask anyone who declined |

Total for a list of about 30 names: 6-10 weeks. Hold a send if a Messenger warning appears; wait 24 hours before resuming. If a posted review is not visible after 72 hours, pause new asks for two weeks and check the profile for a review restriction.

## Before the first message

1. Get the direct review link from the Business Profile dashboard ("Ask for reviews"; support.google.com/business/answer/3474122, platform_primary, snippet-confirmed; re-verify on the live page). Use the same link for everyone. Do not wrap it in a third-party shortener.
2. Test the link from a phone while logged into a personal Google account: it must open the review box directly.
3. Confirm the profile is not under a review restriction (dashboard notice).
4. Build the tracker below in the private workspace with every eligible name filled in and the personal detail per row written before any send. Writing the detail first is what prevents template messages.
5. Decide who answers replies within the hour during the day. Some replies will be a booking request; route those to the normal intake.

## Message set

Every message is personal; these are shapes, not scripts. Swap the bracketed parts for the real detail, and vary the opening line from message to message so no two are identical. Keep the ask to one sentence and the link on its own line.

**Opener shapes (rotate)**

- "Hi [first name], it's [owner first name]. I was thinking about [the real detail: your place on [street], the move-out clean before the baby came, the years I did the Friday cleans] and it made me smile."
- "[First name]! Long time. I still remember [the real detail]."
- "Hi [first name], hope you and [family or pet detail] are doing well. Quick one from me."

**The ask (one sentence, then the link)**

- "I finally set up [Business name] on Google and I have zero reviews there, so if you'd be willing to write a couple of honest sentences about your experience, even if it was a while back, it would help me more than you know."
- "[Link]"
- "Totally fine if not, and it's fine to say how long ago it was. Either way it's good to see your name."

**Nudge (one only, day 5-7, only if no reply)**

- "No pressure at all, [first name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Here's the link again: [Link]. Thank you either way."

**Thank-you (within 24 hours of the review appearing)**

- "Saw your review, thank you. That genuinely made my week." Then reply to the review on Google the same day.

**If they reply "what should I write?"**

- "Honestly, two sentences about what you remember is perfect. Whatever's true."

**If they reply with a booking request**

- Thank them, move the conversation to the normal intake (phone, text, or quote form), and log it as a lead with source "past customer, review outreach." Do not quote in Messenger unless the approved price formula allows it.

**If they decline or go quiet after the nudge**

- One warm close: "Of course, thanks for reading. Take care." Mark the row closed. Never ask again.

## Tracker columns

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| First name, last initial | Full names and profile links stay in the private workspace |
| Eligible (Y/N) and reason | Paid customer; not family, staff, or partner |
| Period served and personal detail | Written before the first send |
| Priority (1 recent and warm, 2 older, 3 uncertain) | Work 1s first |
| Send date and opener used | Rotate openers; confirm no two identical messages in a day |
| Reply date and reply type | Yes, question, booking request, decline, none |
| Nudge date | Only one, day 5-7 |
| Review posted date and stars | From the profile, not from the reply |
| Review visible after 72 hours (Y/N) | Filtered reviews are the pacing signal |
| Owner reply to review posted (date) | Same day |
| Thank-you sent (date) | Within 24 hours |
| Status | Open, waiting, reviewed, declined, closed |

## Weekly check (Monday scorecard rows)

- Asks sent this week and to date
- Reviews posted this week and to date, and how many are visible after 72 hours
- Reply rate and review rate on asked names (counts, not percentages, below 25 asks)
- Median hours from review to owner reply
- Booking requests that came out of the outreach
- Any Messenger warning or Google review restriction (stop signal)

Published response-rate benchmarks are self-reported survey figures and are not forecasts. Measure your own list.
