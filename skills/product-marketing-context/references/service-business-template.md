# Product Marketing Context Template: Local Service Business

**How to use this file.** Copy it into the client's private workspace as `.claude/product-marketing-context.md`. Never copy a filled version into the shared skill library, and never load another client's copy into this session. Every field defaults to **"Unknown; do not publish"** until the owner supplies the fact. Tag each entry with an evidence class (1 verified business fact, 2 direct customer evidence, 3 hypothesis to test, 4 unknown; do not publish as fact) and a date. Text in [brackets] is a prompt for what to capture; delete the prompt when you fill the field. Filling this file is not a publish, spend, send, or price decision. Those still pass through approval gates B (publish), C (spend), D (send templates), and E (price or offer).

Mirror the SaaS template in `SKILL.md` where the sections overlap. Sections that only apply to software (B2B personas, switching from competitor software, customer logos) are dropped here on purpose.
