# Sample Resume: Ideal EA — Commercial Real Estate

> **Note:** This is a fictional example designed to illustrate best practices for EA/CRE resumes. "Jordan Ellis" is not a real person.

---

## What Makes This Resume Work

Before the resume itself, here's why each choice was made:

- **Summary leads with value, not biography** — tells the employer what they get, not what Jordan has done
- **Every bullet answers "so what?"** — responsibilities are translated into business outcomes
- **Quantified results throughout** — deal volume, transaction counts, principals supported signal scale
- **Technology listed as a section** — CRE employers scan for software fluency fast
- **AI framing is honest and specific** — mentions real tools and real use cases, not a buzzword
- **One page fits 0–8 years; two pages is fine for 9+** — Jordan uses two pages without padding

## Resume Annotations

### Summary
"Known for keeping complex deal pipelines organized and clients well-served — without requiring hand-holding" is the line that wins interviews. It speaks directly to the principal's unspoken fear: *will I have to manage this person?*

### Quantification Strategy
Not every role has perfect metrics. Here's how Jordan handles it:
- **Senior role:** Has real numbers ($180M volume, 15–20 deals, 400+ contacts)
- **Mid role:** Uses proxy metrics ("60+ transactions annually," "80+ active listings") and outcome language ("near zero" errors)
- **Entry role:** Honest about scope — support functions, no inflated claims

If you don't have exact numbers, use ranges and honest qualifiers ("approximately," "an estimated"). Never fabricate.

### AI Proficiency Line
> "Use ChatGPT and Claude daily to draft client correspondence, summarize lengthy due diligence documents, and create first drafts of property descriptions; currently building proficiency in AI-assisted data organization."

This is the model sentence structure:
1. Specific tools by name
2. Specific use cases (not "used AI for productivity")
3. Honest signal of growth ("currently building") — not overclaiming expertise

### The Honest AI Section
In the Technology & Tools section:
> "ChatGPT, Claude — daily use for drafting, summarizing, and research"

Short. Specific. Honest. The daily use + specific verbs do the work. Avoid listing AI tools you've only used once or twice.
