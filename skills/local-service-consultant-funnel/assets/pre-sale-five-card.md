# Pre-Sale Five Card

A 3x5 index card that lives in the consultant's pocket. Front: the five questions, in order. Back: the four ethics lines and the season line, initialed.

## Front

**Ask, in this order. Write his answers down after, not during.**

1. What's the slowest part of getting paid?
2. When does it go quiet for you?
3. How do people usually reach you the first time, and who answers when you're on a ladder?
4. What does a typical job run, roughly? (Ask only after he holds the page or a blank sheet is in your hand. Under about $3,000 means the offer is the Phone and Reviews Afternoon, not the quote sheet.)
5. How many quotes went out last month, and how many said yes? (Same rule as 4.)

His answers are the best evidence you will ever have. Label them owner-stated. They outrank every number in every reference file.

## Back

**Initial each line before the first conversation.**

- ____ I never work from a document he did not hand me. A customer's copy, a relative's copy, a counter clerk's copy: out.
- ____ I never hold a login. If he wants help on a profile, he adds me on his own account and keeps ownership.
- ____ Nothing goes out in his name without a standing written OK and a per-message yes. I text him only after he texts me.
- ____ Nothing is published, quoted, or shown to anyone without his written OK.
- ____ Season: no pitch to an exterior painter on a job site, and none in his prime weeks. Rain days and evenings only. Other trades on the slow week they name.

## Card spec

Print or hand-write. Two cards: one in the truck, one in the folder with the one-pagers.
