# Follow-Up Card and Quote Log

Two pieces the client keeps in the truck. The card tells him what to send when a quote goes quiet. The log is the only number a later write-up can carry, and it is his.

## The three-touch card (client-facing)

**When a quote goes quiet**

**Day 2, a call.** "Just checking you got the quote and seeing if anything on it needs explaining."

**Day 7, a text or a drop-by, with one useful thing.** "Here's a photo of a [deck, porch, room] I finished last week with the same product. If you want a look at it in person, the owner said yes."

**Day 14, the close-the-file note, by text or mail.** "I'll hold the price on your quote until [date]. No pressure either way. If the timing isn't right this season, I'm glad to look again in the spring."

Then stop. One more touch a season later, only if he has something real to say (a date that opened, a price that changed).

Rules printed at the bottom of the card: real reasons only; no countdowns; no "only two spots left"; the customer can say stop and it stops.

## The one-line quote log (client-facing)

| Quote no. | Date | Customer initials | Amount | Source | Status | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Follow-up 3 | Signed date | Deposit taken Y/N | Balance paid at walkthrough Y/N |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | | | | |

Status: sent, yes, no, quiet.

Source: call, text, referral by first name, drove by the sign, online.

Initials only. No customer names, addresses, or phone numbers in the log. It is a count, not a customer list.

## Why the log exists

- It is the one number that can ever go in a write-up, with his written OK: quotes sent, quotes signed, deposits taken, balances paid at the walkthrough.
- It decides the Season Tune-Up: in March the log says whether anything changed. Nothing else does.
- Figures about follow-up from software vendors (30 to 35% close with follow-up against 12 to 18% without; JobEstimator and Level CFO, vendor, low confidence, retrieved 2026-09-15, not read at source) are hypotheses to test against this log, never a promise.

## How the consultant uses it

- At the day 20 or 30 check-in, read it together. Count only.
- Photograph it with his OK, numbers only, initials covered, for the results ledger.
- If the log is empty after 30 days, the product was delivered and not adopted. Log that as evidence too, and ask what got in the way.
