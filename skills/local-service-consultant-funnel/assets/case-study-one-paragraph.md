# Case Study, One Paragraph

A one-paragraph write-up with every sentence labeled. Written only after the permission form is signed, at the level it allows. No adjectives, no growth claims, no numbers beyond what the level permits. Counts, never ratios, under a client's name.

## The template

> [Trade] in [town] (permission level [A, B, or C], [date]). **Before (FACT, [date]):** quotes went out on [a pad, by text] with [no total, no start window, no deposit line, no place to sign]; [count] reviews at [rating], [count] unanswered. **Delivered ([date]):** [the rebuilt quote sheet, the two-sentence script, the truck card, the one-line log, the one-page review]. **He said (YOU SAID, [date]):** "[his sentence, approved by him in writing]." **The log shows (COUNTS, [date range]):** [n] quotes sent, [n] signed, [n] deposits taken at signing, [n] balances paid at the walkthrough. **Permission:** level [A, B, or C], signed [date]. Nothing was given in exchange for this write-up.

## Example, watermarked EXAMPLE

> A painter in a mountain county (permission level B, [date]). **Before (FACT, [date]):** quotes went out on a carbon pad with no total, no start window, no deposit line, and no place to sign; four reviews at 3.0, two unanswered. **Delivered ([date]):** the rebuilt quote sheet, the two-sentence script, the truck card, the one-line log, the one-page review. **He said (YOU SAID, [date]):** "[his sentence]." **The log shows (COUNTS, [date range]):** [n] quotes sent, [n] signed, [n] deposits taken at signing, [n] balances paid at the walkthrough. **Permission:** level B, signed [date]. Nothing was given in exchange for this write-up.

## Rules

- Level A allows a blank before-and-after layout only, with the business name, numbers, and customers covered. No paragraph.
- Level B allows first name, trade, town, and counts. No dollar amounts. No ratios.
- Level C allows the business name and a sentence in his words that he approved in writing first.
- "Before" is what the pocket read and his own sheet showed, dated. Nothing from any document he did not hand over.
- If anything was ever given in exchange, the last sentence changes to a plain disclosure: "[First name] received [what] in connection with this work."
- The write-up is shown to the next prospect on paper, in person, never posted anywhere without the level allowing it.
