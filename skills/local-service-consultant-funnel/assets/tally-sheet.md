## The Friday ritual

1. Fill the row from the touch log, the referrer log, and the hours and mileage log.
2. Compare selling hours to building hours. After selling starts, selling is at least 70% of business hours.
3. Read the stop rules below against the row.
4. Write one sentence: what changes next week.

## The 70/30 rule

Before the first conversation, 50/50 between building and selling. From the first conversation on, at least 70% of business hours go to conversations and follow-ups. Any week building beats selling, cut the build.

## Stop rules

- 10 conversations and zero sheets seen: change the ask, not the product.
- 5 offers on paper and zero signed: change the trade or the scope, not the price.
- Any week building hours beat selling hours: cut the build.
- Fewer than 15 names with a venue by the end of week one: the market size is the finding. Decide that day on radius or remote.
- Deadline minus 30 days with no offer on paper: stop all building.

## Cash rules

- Cash out before the first signature is capped at one sale's worth. Over the ceiling: stop spending.
- Any software that bills monthly gets a keep-or-pause decision at the deadline minus 30 days if it is not live and earning.
- Line six of the five-decision sheet (the month the practice must pay you) is checked every Friday against the signed column.
