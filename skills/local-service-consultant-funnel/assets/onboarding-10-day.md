# Onboarding, 10 Days

One page. It starts at one gate, delivers one page he can hold inside ten days, and turns the check-in into the second-sale conversation. The gate is signature plus payment. Nothing starts before both.

## The four promises (on the welcome sheet)

1. I never ask for a password.
2. Nothing goes out in your name without your OK.
3. Nothing is published without your written OK.
4. No guarantee, and nothing renews.

## The plan

| Day | Step | Output | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Signature and payment at the table. Hand over the welcome sheet (assets/welcome-sheet.md). | Signed letter, receipt, welcome sheet in his hands | Both |
| 0 to 2 | Intake, 20 minutes, in person or by phone (assets/intake-15.md). Ask "what would make this a waste of your money?" and write it word for word. | Intake done; acceptance criterion recorded | Consultant asks, he answers |
| 3 | Draft the sheet, the script, the card, the log, and the one page. Send a photo by text or bring it by. | Draft seen | Consultant |
| 5 | His one round of changes. One nudge if silent. | Changes in | Him |
| 7 | Delivery in person. The print-ready file and a texted copy, the script on the back, the truck card, the log, the one page. Write one real quote together on the new sheet. | Delivered | Both |
| 10 | "Used it on a quote yet?" One text, only if he has texted you first; otherwise wait for the check-in. | Logged | Consultant |
| 20 or 30 | Check-in (assets/kickoff-and-checkin-agenda.md): log read together, permission answered, referral ask with three cards, tune-up date on his calendar, report-back to the referrer. | Reference exists or it does not | Both |

Choose day 20 if the signature landed inside the last month of a season or close to a deadline; day 30 otherwise.

## Delivered means

The pad file, the card, the log, and the one page are in his hands, and a walkthrough was held or offered twice. Delivery is defined by the consultant's own acts. What he does with it afterward is logged as evidence, not treated as acceptance.

## Two people to invite

- Whoever answers the phone (often a spouse or bookkeeper). Half the paperwork lands on that desk. Name them in intake question 15 and invite them to the delivery visit.
- Nobody else. No helpers, no customers.

## The trial pad rule

Before a pad of fifty is printed, one real quote is written on a 10-sheet trial pad with the homeowner script said out loud. What the customer says back goes in the script log.

## What is not in these ten days

Software setup, ads, a website, a second product, or any talk of the next rung. The next rung is raised only at the check-in, and only if the log gives a reason.
