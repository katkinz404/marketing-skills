# Consultant Truth Ledger

The seven-column ledger from local-service-sales-page, pointed at the consultant's own business. Every statement that could reach a prospect, a page, or a letter gets a row. On day one the ledger has one verified fact. Everything else is a hold with an owner.

Evidence classes, exactly four: verified business fact, direct customer evidence, hypothesis to test, unknown (do not publish as fact).

Publication status: allowed, conditional, hold, or private.

## Rules for this ledger

- Read and update it every Monday with the tally sheet.
- A prospect's spoken number is direct customer evidence, labeled owner-stated until a document confirms it.
- Industry research never becomes a fact about the consultant or a client by repetition.
- Anything still Unknown on the day a page publishes stays off the page.
