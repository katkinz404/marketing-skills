# Welcome Sheet: What Happens Next

Handed over at the table the moment the letter is signed and the payment is in. One page. Plain words. He keeps it on the dash.

## The sheet

**[Business name]** for **[his business name]**

Paid on [date]. Thank you. Here is what happens next, on one page.

**Four promises**

1. I never ask for a password.
2. Nothing goes out in your name without your OK.
3. Nothing gets published without your written OK.
4. No guarantee, and nothing renews on its own.

**The next ten days**

- Days 0 to 2: we talk for 20 minutes. I ask for nine numbers and one question: what would make this a waste of your money.
- Day 3: you see a draft, by text or in person.
- Day 5: your one round of changes.
- Day 7: I hand you the page in person, and we write one real quote on it together.
- Day 20 or 30: we read your quote log together for 30 minutes.

**What I need from you, one list, once**

- A blank quote sheet, or a photo of one.
- 20 minutes for the nine numbers.
- The name of whoever answers your phone.
- Your changes by day 5.

**How to reach me**

Call or text [phone]. Email [email]. I reply [reply window]. If I said Thursday, it will be Thursday.

**If it's late**

If the page is not in your hands within 10 business days after I have your blank sheet, your numbers, and your payment, you get every dollar back.

I am not a lawyer, CPA, or insurance adviser. Any legal, tax, or insurance line on your paperwork is yours to source.

## Notes for the consultant (not printed)

- Print it on the same card stock as the one-pager. It should look like it came from the same person.
- The reply window is the one from the five-decision sheet. Print only a window you can keep on a bad week.
