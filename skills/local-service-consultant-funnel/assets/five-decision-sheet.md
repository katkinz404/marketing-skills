# Five-Decision Sheet

Fill this in one sitting before the first conversation. It is the consultant's own version of the test she sells: the paperwork has to be right before the pitch. Nothing else in this skill is honest until these six lines have an answer.

| # | Decision | Answer | Date decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name on the invoice and on the check. Legal name as a sole proprietor, or a trade name (which needs an assumed business name filing before invoice one) | [name] | [date] |
| 2 | Where the money lands: the account, and the path for a check or cash at the table (numbered receipt book) | [account], [receipt book yes or no] | [date] |
| 3 | Price floor per hour and per project, and the first price on the one-pager | [floor per hour], [floor per project], first price [default $300] | [date] |
| 4 | Service radius: the counties or towns you will drive to, and whether remote clients are in | [counties], remote [yes or no] | [date] |
| 5 | Reply window and hours you can keep on a bad week, and the two early counter mornings | [reply window], [hours per week], [mornings] | [date] |
| 6 | Money: monthly fixed cost of the practice, months of runway, and the month the practice must pay you | [fixed cost per month], [months of runway], [month it must pay you] | [date] |

## The runway test

Read line six against the first-sale calendar (the first signature is realistic about five weeks after the first conversation; the second sale lands a month or more after that; nothing pays before then).

| If line six says | Then |
|---|---|
| The practice must pay you after the second sale can land | Pass. Run the plan as written. |
| The practice must pay you before the second sale can land | Change the plan before anything else. Pick at least one: raise the first price to [$400] if the first three tailgate conversations do not flinch; sell 1A and 1B to the same client as two separate sales; move the first Season Tune-Up earlier to a log read at the first check-in. Write the change here: [change] |
| Runway is under three months | Stop and say so. A $300-first-sale plan cannot carry a practice with under three months of runway. Decide on income first, then come back. |

Test result: [pass, change, or stop]. Reasoning: [one sentence].

## Paperwork gates

- Checks payable to a personal name repeat the flaw you sell against. If you trade under any name but your own, file the assumed business name before the first invoice. Confirm with a CPA that invoicing under your legal name as a sole proprietor is clean for you.
- Every invoice states consulting on its own line and anything printed or digital on another line. Tax treatment of each line is the CPA's call; leave it as [per CPA] until answered.
- Mileage and hours log starts the day you sign this sheet.
