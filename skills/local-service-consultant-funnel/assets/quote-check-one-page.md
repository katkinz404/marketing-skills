## Back

**How the rows are ordered.** By dollars to you, not by topic. Your own numbers outrank anything from outside. Free fixes come first. Ties go to the cheaper fix. Rows with no dollar figure go last.

**The four formulas, with blanks you can check against your own pad:**

1. Missed calls per week [ ] x close rate [ ] x average job $[ ] = $[ ] a week.
2. Quotes per month [ ] x (close rate with follow-up [ ] minus close rate today [ ]) x average job $[ ] = $[ ] a month. Both close rates are yours; the difference is a hypothesis until your log shows it.
3. Dollars waiting on final checks $[ ] x days waiting [ ] = the money you are lending your customers.
4. Referral share of jobs [ ] x jobs per year [ ] x average job $[ ] = what a working referral ask is worth.

**Label key, one sentence.** FACT is something we both saw with a date on it; YOU SAID is your number, taken at your word; HYPOTHESIS is my reasoning that your log will confirm or kill; UNKNOWN means neither of us knows yet.

**Where outside figures come from.** Any figure not yours is marked "one national vendor's figure" and is context, not a forecast about your business.

## Rules for the writer (not printed)

- Banned words on this page: poor, weak, bad, unprofessional, losing, bleeding, guaranteed, must.
- Never a statute, a tax rule, an industry standard number, a competitor's name, or a comment on his cash price.
- Rows come from the pocket read (assets/quote-check-pocket-read.md) plus the intake numbers (assets/intake-15.md). The quote-sheet row is scored only from a sheet he handed you.
- Vendor figures that may appear as "one national vendor's figure": about 27% of home-services calls go unanswered and about $1,200 of value per missed call (Invoca, vendor, cited as 2024, retrieved 2026-09-15, not read at source); 30 to 35% close with follow-up against 12 to 18% without (JobEstimator and Level CFO, vendor, low confidence, not read at source). Recheck before printing.
- Page one is one page. If it does not fit, cut a row, not the type size.
