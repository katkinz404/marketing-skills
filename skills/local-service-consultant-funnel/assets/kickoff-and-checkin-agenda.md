# Kickoff and Check-In Agendas

Two half-page agendas. The kickoff is the intake call. The check-in is the visit that creates the reference and opens the second sale.

## Kickoff and intake (20 minutes, day 0 to 2)

| Minute | Item | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | The four promises, said out loud once. | He has heard them |
| 2 to 14 | The nine numbers (assets/intake-15.md, layer B), read back one at a time. | Intake sheet, every number owner-stated |
| 14 to 16 | "What would make this a waste of your money?" Written word for word. | Acceptance criterion |
| 16 to 18 | The blank quote sheet in hand, or the photo he sent. Confirm the lines he actually fills in. | Rebuild inputs |
| 18 to 20 | Who answers the phone; who is invited to delivery; the delivery date and place. | Day 7 set |

Logged after: intake done, delivery date, who attends.

## Check-in (30 minutes, day 20 or 30)

| Minute | Item | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Read the quote log together. Count only: sent, signed, deposits taken, balances paid at the walkthrough. Ask what got in the way of any line he stopped filling in. | Counts in the results ledger |
| 10 to 15 | The permission question (assets/proof-permission-form.md). Record yes or no and the level. Never at the moment of payment; payment was weeks ago. | Permission answered |
| 15 to 22 | The referral ask: "Who else writes quotes on a pad?" Hand him three one-pagers with his name on the "sent by" line. | Names, or no names |
| 22 to 27 | The Season Tune-Up date on his calendar (assets/season-tune-up-checklist.md), only if the log gives a reason. If it does not, say so. | Date, or a plain no |
| 27 to 30 | Anything he wants changed on the sheet now that it has been used. | Notes for the file |

Logged after: counts, permission level, referral names, tune-up date, and a report-back to the referrer within 14 days.

## Rules

- The check-in is a read, not a pitch. The tune-up is raised once, with the log as the reason, and dropped if he says not now.
- Nothing about the next rung above the tune-up is mentioned unless he asks.
- If the log is empty, the product was delivered and not adopted. Ask why, write it down, and treat it as the most useful thing learned that month.
