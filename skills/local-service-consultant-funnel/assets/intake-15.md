# Intake, 15 Questions

Asked on the delivery visit or in one 20-minute call, after signature and payment. The consultant types and reads each answer back. Every number is labeled owner-stated until a document confirms it. No customer names, no logins, no bank details.

## Layer A: the business (reuses the fields of local-service-sales-page's client intake)

| # | Question | Why it matters | What it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What do you do, and where? (Services, and the towns or counties you'll drive to.) | Scope and geography for the sheet header | Quote sheet header, profile |
| 2 | How many jobs a week can you take right now, and with whom? (Just you, or a helper?) | Capacity; who is on site | Start-window wording, insurance question sheet |
| 3 | How do customers find you today? (Referrals, sign, drove by, online, other.) | Where the work comes from | Pocket read row 3, referral ask |
| 4 | How do they reach you the first time, and who answers when you're on a ladder? | The phone leak | Missed-call text-back, review row |
| 5 | How do you take payment today, and when? (Cash, check, card; at the end, halfway, at signing.) | Deposit and balance lines | Quote sheet money block |
| 6 | Who approves anything I might show someone, and what may never be said about your business? | Publication authority; prohibited claims | Proof permission form, case-study rules |

## Layer B: the nine numbers no template asks

| # | Question | Why it matters | What it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | What does a typical job run, roughly? | Sizes everything against a tenth of one job | Price sanity, formulas |
| 8 | How many quotes went out last month, and how many said yes? | The only baseline the log can be compared to | Quote log, row 1 of the page |
| 9 | Where did the last five jobs come from? | Real source mix, not the guessed one | Referral ask, formulas |
| 10 | How many jobs a week could you take if the phone rang more? | Whether more quotes would even help | Honest scoping; the anti-profile check |
| 11 | Which months are busy, and which are slow? | Season lane; tune-up dates | Calendar, tune-up |
| 12 | Of the last ten jobs, how many paid cash, check, card? | Payment methods line | Quote sheet, receipt path |
| 13 | From the day a job is done, how many days until the final check clears, usually? | The final-check leak | Balance-at-walkthrough line, formula 3 |
| 14 | How many calls do you miss in a normal week? | The phone leak | Formula 1, text-back |
| 15 | Who signs the quote, and who answers the phone? (You, a spouse, a bookkeeper?) | Who the paperwork lands on; who to invite to delivery | Delivery visit, 1B |

## The last question

"What would make this a waste of your money?" Write the answer word for word. It is the acceptance criterion he just handed you.

## Rules

- Label each number owner-stated. Replace the label only when a document (a pad, a bank line he shows you) confirms it.
- Do not ask for customer names, addresses, logins, or bank details. The count of finished customers in the last 90 days is fine; the names never.
- Read the sheet back before you leave. His correction is the most valuable minute of the visit.
