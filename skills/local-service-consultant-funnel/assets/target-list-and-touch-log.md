# Target List and Touch Log

The working list of the first twenty names, in the order you will work them, with a touch log per name. Internal only.

## List rules

A name goes on the list only when all four are true:

1. Owner plus zero to two helpers, one truck, one phone, quotes at the property.
2. A visible trigger: an unanswered negative review, a name or phone mismatch across listings, no way to request a quote without calling, or a referrer's story of a late final check or a quote that went quiet.
3. A typical job of about $3,000 or more, so the first price stays under a tenth of one job. Until he tells you, the ticket column says [ask]. Under $3,000 means the offer is 1B.
4. A slow week he names before your deadline, and a venue: a place and time where you can meet him that is not a customer's job site.

No venue, no name. The venue column is filled before the name goes on the list, not after.

## Order

1. Connectors 1 to 5: the SBDC or SCORE mentor, a paint or lumber counter, the insurance agent who writes contractor policies, a realtor or property manager, one more you already know.
2. Trades that named a slow week inside your window.
3. Exterior painters, on rain days only, until their season closes; then in the first weeks after.

## Week-one gate

Fewer than 15 names with a venue by the end of week one means the market size is the finding. Decide that day whether to widen the radius or take remote clients. Do not pad the list with names you cannot meet.

## Cadence note

Research on enterprise prospecting reports an average of eight touches to a first meeting and five for the best performers (RAIN Group, research firm, retrieved 2026-09-15, not read at source). That is enterprise data. For a tradesman met in person, plan five, make each one useful, and let him end it.

## What counts as a useful thing

- The favor card (one public fact, one thing to do)
- A photo of a similar job's paperwork, blank
- A date that opened up in your week
- The answer to a question he asked last time
- Nothing at all: "I said I'd call Thursday, so I'm calling"
