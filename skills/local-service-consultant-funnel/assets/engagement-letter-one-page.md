# Engagement Letter, One Page

[Have an attorney or the free SBDC or SCORE adviser review this before first use. It is a template in plain words, not legal advice.]

Front and back of one sheet. Signed at the table. Payment is taken at the same sitting for rung 1. The consultant runs the same rule she sells: paid at signing, paperwork first.

## Front

**Agreement between [business name] and [client business name]**

Date: [date]. Client: [client owner name], [client business name], [town]. Consultant: [your name], [business name], [town], [phone].

**What you are buying.** [Rung name, for example Quote Sheet Rebuild.] It includes: [copy the inclusions from the offer card, one per line]. It does not include: [copy the exclusions, one per line].

**Price and payment.** [First price, default $300], paid in full at signing by check payable to [business name], cash with a numbered receipt, or a payment link. [For rung 3: 50% at signing, 50% at the walkthrough.]

**Delivered means.** The [pad file, script card, follow-up card, quote log, and one-page review] are in your hands, and a walkthrough was held or offered twice. What you do with them after is yours.

**Refund.** Full refund if the deliverable is not in your hands within 10 business days after the last of three things: your blank quote sheet, your numbers, and your payment.

**Acceptance.** You accept the work when you initial the proof at the walkthrough. One round of changes is included by day 5 of delivery.

**What I need from you.** One list, once: a blank quote sheet or a photo of one; 20 minutes for your numbers; the name of whoever answers your phone; your one round of changes by day 5.

## Back

**No guarantee.** I do not promise more jobs, more calls, or any number. I will show you your own log.

**Not a lawyer, CPA, or insurance adviser.** Any legal, tax, or insurance line on your documents is yours to source. I leave a labeled blank for it and never fill it.

**Who owns what.** You own every deliverable, in your name, on your accounts. I keep my blank templates and the right to reuse them.

**Confidentiality.** What you tell me stays between us. Nothing is published, quoted, or shown to anyone without your written OK on the separate permission page.

**Your accounts.** I never ask for a password. If you want help on a profile, you add me on your own login and keep ownership. Nothing goes out in your name without your OK.

**Out of scope.** Ads, search content, software subscriptions, anything monthly, anything that renews, drafting legal or tax text, review rewards or filters, and any work from a document that did not come from you.

**Limit of liability.** My total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid me. Neither of us is liable to the other for indirect or consequential losses.

**Ending it.** Either of us can end this in writing at any time. Work done to that point is paid for; work not done is refunded.

Client signature: ______________________ Date: ________

Consultant signature: __________________ Date: ________
