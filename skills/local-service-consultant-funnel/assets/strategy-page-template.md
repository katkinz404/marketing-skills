# Strategy Page Template

Rung 3, half A. One page for the client, on the five-part structure the U.S. Small Business Administration uses in its marketing plan tool (what you sell, to whom, goals, strategies and tactics, metrics; government, retrieved 2026-09-15, not read at source), with the cost-versus-revenue comparison SCORE puts inside the plan (industry body, retrieved 2026-09-15, not read at source). Every number is his, from the intake or the quote log, labeled owner-stated or from the log.

## The page

**[His business name]: the plan for [season, year]**

**1. What you sell.** [Services, in his words, from intake question 1.] Typical job: $[owner-stated]. Jobs a week you can take: [owner-stated].

**2. Who buys it.** [Where the last five jobs came from, intake question 9.] Who pays how: [cash, check, card mix, intake question 12]. Who answers the phone: [intake question 15].

**3. Goals for the season.** Written as counts he can read off his own log, not percentages:
- Quotes that get an answer: from [n] of [n] last month (owner-stated) to [n] of [n].
- Deposits taken at signing: from [n] to [n].
- Balances paid at the walkthrough: from [n] to [n].
- Days from done to final check: from [n] (owner-stated) to [n].
- Reviews answered: every one, within a week.

**4. What we will do, and what it costs.**

| Week | Action | Owner | Cost | The count it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use the rebuilt sheet on every quote; say the two sentences | Him | $0 | deposits at signing |
| 1 | Truck card: day 2 call, day 7 useful thing, day 14 note | Him | $0 | quotes that get an answer |
| 2 | Missed-call text-back on, in his words | Him, with her beside him | $0 on a free tier | missed calls |
| 2 | Every existing review answered; the neutral ask to every finished customer | Him posts, she drafts | $0 | reviews answered, review count |
| 3 | Referral ask with three cards at every walkthrough | Him | printing [judgment] | referral share of jobs |
| 4 to 12 | Log read together once a month | Both | inside the package | all of the above |

**5. How we will know.** The quote log, read together monthly. Counts only. Nothing in this plan is a guarantee.

**Cost against revenue.** This package: $[price]. One recovered quiet quote at your typical job: $[owner-stated]. The plan pays for itself when the log shows [n] more signed quotes than the month before it started. That is the test, and it is yours to read.

## What is deliberately not in this plan

Ads. Search content. Anything monthly. Any software above a free tier. Any number that is not his. Any claim about more jobs.

## Rules for the writer

- Every figure carries owner-stated or from-the-log. Nothing from a vendor report appears on this page.
- Goals are counts, not percentages, because he reads counts off a pad.
- The cost-against-revenue line names one job, not a projection.
