# Credibility Page Brief

The one screen that goes on the web after a handshake or a referral, so a tradesman can check the consultant is real and tap to call. No form, by design. Follows the page-brief structure in local-service-sales-page. Build cap: four hours. Publish only after the business name is settled and the prelaunch truth gate passes.

## Executive decision

- Recommended page type: one landing page, no form, no thank-you page
- One audience: owner-operator trades within [service area] who quote in person
- One triggering job: "someone handed me a card or mentioned her; is she real, and how do I reach her?"
- One primary action: tap to call or text
- Actual next state: a call or a text to the consultant, answered inside [reply window]
- Confidence: high on the layout; every fact about the consultant is on hold until the truth ledger clears
- Largest unresolved risk: no traffic, by design; the page records nothing if cards are not handed out

## Traffic and message match

- Source: the one-pager handed over in person; a referrer mentioning her name
- Source promise: "one page between your quote and your last check," [first price] once, no monthly
- Visitor intent: verify, then call
- Page promise: the same sentence, the same price, the same five promises, the same phone number

## Offer card

- Audience: owner plus zero to two helpers, quotes at the property, typical job about $3,000 or more
- Trigger: a quote that went quiet, a late final check, an unanswered review, a card in his hand
- Outcome: one page he can hold that fixes the paperwork between the handshake and the last check
- Service: the Quote Sheet Rebuild or the Phone and Reviews Afternoon
- Inclusions: the rebuilt sheet, the script, the truck card, the log, the one-page review, one real quote together
- Exclusions: anything monthly, ads, software subscriptions, logins, legal or tax text
- Process: hand over a blank sheet or a photo; 20 minutes; the page in [turnaround] business days
- Proof: none invented; process clarity, owner visibility, a published price, the straight-talk line
- Price: [first price, default $300], paid once
- Risk reduction: the five promises and the refund clock
- Customer responsibilities: a blank sheet, 20 minutes, one round of changes
- Geography: [service area]
- Capacity: rain days and evenings in season; weekday mornings off-season
- CTA: Call, Text
- Next state: a call or text answered inside [reply window]

## Hero directions

### Direction 1 (verified)
- Eyebrow: For painters, pressure washers, handymen, and small remodelers who quote in person
- Headline: One page between your quote and your last check.
- Subheadline: I rewrite the paperwork a trade hands a customer: the quote sheet, the deposit terms, and what you send when a quote goes quiet. [First price], paid once. No monthly. No contract. I never ask for a password.
- CTA: Call [phone], Text
- Microcopy: I reply [reply window].
- Image: the blank sheet

### Direction 2 (evidence-informed)
- Headline: The quote that went quiet is money you already earned.
- Subheadline: I fix what happens between the handshake and the last check. One page, [first price], once.
- Why not first: it leans on a claim about his quotes before he has said it himself.

### Direction 3 (hypothesis)
- Headline: Half when we start. The rest when you've walked it with me.
- Subheadline: The two sentences that get a deposit at the table and the balance at the walkthrough, printed on your pad.
- Why not first: it opens on the customer script, which he has not seen yet.

### Recommendation
- Selected: Direction 1
- Reason: it repeats the card in his hand word for word, which is the page's only job
- What would change it: three referrers reporting that tradesmen asked "what does she actually do?"

## Contact section, and why there is no form

A tradesman on a phone in a truck taps to call. A form asks him to type and wait. The form belongs to the full funnel (assets/full-funnel-page-brief.md), which is built only when the two triggers trip. Until then the page's plumbing is a phone that is answered.

## Leadpages build map

- Surface: landing page (one page; no thank-you page because there is no form)
- Brand kit: none approved; neutral structure, one accent color, one display face and one body face
- Section order: header, hero, the sheet, five promises, how it works, who, where and when, contact, footer
- Components: tap-to-call and tap-to-text buttons (44 by 44 CSS pixels or larger), one image slot for the real photo, one image or HTML block for the blank sheet
- Integrations: none
- Mobile: single column; test on a real phone on a cellular connection
- Accessibility: visible labels, 4.5:1 contrast, visible keyboard focus, alternative text on the photo and the sheet image
- Performance: one font family per role, compressed images, no autoplay, no third-party scripts
- Analytics: phone_click and text_click, plus the lead log kept by hand; no personal data in any event or URL
- Content and claims to exclude: any price above the first price; statistics; testimonials; star counts; countdowns; stock photos; a fictional filled-in example

## Launch status

- Truth gate: fail until the Hold rows clear: business name, price, turnaround, service area, photo, phone, postal address if email is sent
- Conversion plumbing: pass once the phone number on the page is the one that is answered
- Accessibility: to be tested on the published URL
- Performance: to be tested on the published URL on a phone
- Publication approval: not requested
- Final status: no-go today; conditional go the day the Hold rows clear
