# Season Tune-Up Checklist

Rung 2. [Price, default $500] per visit, paid at the visit. Six to eight hours of the consultant's time. Twice a year, in the slow weeks before each season. Nothing renews; the next date goes on his calendar at the previous check-in.

## Before the visit (2 hours)

- [ ] Read the quote log photos he sent, numbers only.
- [ ] Rescore the six pocket-read rows (assets/quote-check-pocket-read.md) with today's date.
- [ ] Note what changed since the last page, in his numbers.
- [ ] Draft replies for any review posted since the last visit.

## The visit (4 to 5 hours, at his table)

- [ ] Read the quote log together. Count: sent, signed, deposits taken, balances paid at the walkthrough.
- [ ] Update the quote sheet: prices, materials line, start-window wording, anything he stopped filling in.
- [ ] Refresh the profile with his photos, him logged in, consultant beside him.
- [ ] He posts the drafted review replies.
- [ ] The referral push: three cards, the "who else writes quotes on a pad?" ask, never at the moment of payment.
- [ ] One real quote written together on the updated sheet.
- [ ] Set the next tune-up date on his calendar.

## After the visit (1 hour)

- [ ] A one-page update in the same five-row format, dated, with what changed.
- [ ] Results ledger entry, counts only.
- [ ] Report back to any referrer whose name came up.

## What he holds

The rescored page, the updated sheet file (and a print spec if a reprint is due), the replies posted, the next date.

## Pricing note

At the visit, by check or cash, receipt on the spot. Nothing renews on its own. If the log shows nothing changed and nothing needs changing, say so and charge for the read, not for work that was not needed.

## The objection you will hear in March

"Nothing looks broken." The honest answer: the log decides. If the log shows every quote signed and every balance paid at the walkthrough, the tune-up is a short visit and a small bill. If it shows quiet quotes and late checks, that is the work.
