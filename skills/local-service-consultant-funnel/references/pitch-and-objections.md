# Pitch and Objections

Internal only. Never shown to a prospect. The talk track for the first five touches, the straight-talk line, and the honest answer to every objection you will hear. Every answer is under 60 words and none of them cites a study, a statute, or a competitor.

## Words

| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| quote, the pad, quote sheet | system, funnel, CRM, pipeline |
| deposit, final check, walkthrough | onboarding, retainer, subscription, monthly |
| a quote that went quiet | audit, founding client |
| answer a review, the phone | leads guaranteed, double your close rate, ROI |
| one price, paid once, you own it | any statute, tax rule, cash price comment, competitor's name |

## The talk track

**Touch 1, the first two questions.** "I'm [first name], I'm local. I fix what happens between the handshake and the last check. Two questions, if you've got a minute: what's the slowest part of getting paid, and when does it go quiet for you?" Then the card and the one-pager. Then leave.

**The favor.** "I looked you up before I came by. Your Yelp page has a different phone number than your sign. Here's the card with both written down, so you can fix whichever one is wrong." One fact, one thing to do, framed as your own experience. Nothing asked.

**The blank sheet.** "Can I see a blank one?" Then the tailgate read, three sentences: "Here's what a homeowner sees. Here's what she can't tell from it: the total, when you start, what happens if she says yes. And here's where the money usually gets lost, at the last check."

**Typical job, before any price.** "What does a typical job run, roughly?" Under about $3,000, the offer is the Phone and Reviews Afternoon.

**The offer on paper.** "Here's the one thing I'd do first. [First price], once. You own it. No monthly. Take it home. I'll call Thursday."

**The close-the-file note, day 9.** "I said I'd stop if it wasn't the right time, so this is me stopping. The offer stands through [date]. If a quote goes quiet on you this month, you have my number."

## The straight-talk line

Say it early, before he asks: "I'm new at this as a business. I don't have another contractor's results to show you. I'll show you your own numbers first." It costs a few buyers who want a track record. It keeps every buyer who has been burned by a promise.

## Objections and honest answers

**I'm booked solid.** "Then this isn't for this month. When does it go quiet? I'll call that week and not before."

**I don't do email.** "Nothing here needs it. A blank sheet in your hand or a photo by text. The page comes back on paper."

**My customers pay cash.** "Good. The sheet keeps cash and check first. It adds a deposit line and a line for the balance at the walkthrough, so nobody has to remember what was agreed."

**My customers are older and like paper.** "So is the sheet. It's paper, in your name, from the same kind of pad. The only new thing is two sentences you say when you hand it over."

**Why you?** "Because I'm holding the only thing that matters, your quote sheet, and I'm not asking you for anything today."

**The last marketing guy took my money.** "What did he sell you? [Listen.] I sell one page, once, and you own it. If it's not in your hands in ten business days, you get every dollar back."

**$300 for a piece of paper?** "For the page, the two sentences that get a deposit at the table, the card for quiet quotes, the log, and one real quote written together. If one quiet quote comes back, it paid for itself. That's your number to check, not mine."

**I'll think about it.** "Take it home. I'll call Thursday. If Thursday's a no, that's a fine answer."

**Can you guarantee it?** "No. Anyone who does is guessing. What I can do is read your log with you in a month and let it speak."

**Can you do it cheaper?** "The price is the price for this page. If the page is more than you need, the phone-and-reviews afternoon is the same price and a different fix. If neither fits, no hard feelings."

**Can you just post for me?** "No. Nothing goes out in your name from my hands. I'll draft, you post, from your own login."

**Can you get me reviews?** "I can get you asking, the same way, every time, with nothing attached. The reviews are up to your customers."

**What about a website?** "Later, if the log says you need one. Right now the page that matters is the one your customer signs."

**I already use [software].** "Keep it. This fits beside it. The sheet is paper; the software can hold the copy."

## The three sentences never said

- Any statute number, rule, or the word misdemeanor.
- Any comment on his cash price, and any hint that it is a tax matter.
- Any competitor's name, and any line that starts with "your competitor is."

## When he says stop

Stop. Log it. Send nothing else. Report back to whoever gave you his name within 14 days. Put his name on the March file only if he said "not now," never if he said no.
