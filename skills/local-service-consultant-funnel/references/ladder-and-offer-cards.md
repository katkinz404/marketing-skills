# Ladder and Offer Cards

One offer card per rung in the format from local-service-sales-page, the pricing rationale in one tradesman sentence, the hours arithmetic, the comparable-price table, and the discount and pilot policy. Every price in the ladder is judgment pending the consultant's price floor; every market figure was retrieved by web search on 2026-09-15 and not read at source.

## Rung 0: the favor

| Component | Answer |
|---|---|
| Audience | Any owner-operator trade on the target list |
| Trigger | The first or second touch |
| Job | Decide whether this person is worth twenty minutes |
| Outcome | One thing he can fix today |
| Service | One index card: one public fact about his business and one thing to do about it; a verbal read of his blank sheet at the tailgate |
| Inclusions | The card, the read |
| Exclusions | Anything written beyond the card; anything from a document he did not hand over |
| Method | Public facts only, 45 minutes of the consultant's time |
| Proof | none; the card is the proof |
| Price path | Free, nothing asked |
| Risk reduction | Nothing to sign, nothing to pay |
| Customer role | Keep the card |
| Action and next state | None. He keeps the card, or not |

## Rung 1A: Quote Sheet Rebuild

| Component | Answer |

## Rung 1B: Phone and Reviews Afternoon

Same card as 1A except: audience is any trade, including typical jobs under $3,000; service is every existing review answered (consultant drafts, he posts), the neutral ask card, a missed-call text-back set up with him typing, the one-page review; delivery is one afternoon; price [first price, default $300], 100% at signing.

## Rung 2: Season Tune-Up

Audience: a rung 1 client. Trigger: the slow weeks before a season, and a log that shows something. Service: rescored page, log read together, sheet and prices updated, profile refreshed with him logged in, review replies drafted, referral push, one real quote together. Price [default $500] per visit, paid at the visit, nothing renews. Next state: the next date on his calendar, or a plain no.

## Rung 3: Quote-to-Check Package

Audience: a rung 1 client who asks, in the off-season. Half A (money and phone) [default $850]; half B (the quote page) [default $650]; both [default $1,500]. 50% at signing, 50% at the walkthrough. Scope in assets/quote-to-check-package-sow.md.

## Pricing rationale, one sentence per rung

- Rung 1: "It's a tenth of one job, once, and you own it."
- Rung 2: "You pay for a read, and only when the log gives me something to read."
- Rung 3: "It's one job, split in two, paid half when we start and half when you've seen it work."

## Discount and pilot policy

- Client one may receive a written discount on rung 3 in exchange for permissioned proof only, at the level the permission form allows. Never a credit and a discount stacked on the same sale.
- Never publish a quote-log ratio (signed divided by sent) under a client's name in a small county; counts only, at level B or C.
- Anything given in exchange for a write-up is disclosed in the write-up.
- Never sold as a headline: Google Business Profile setup (a $97 commodity), anything monthly, anything with a lead guarantee.
- The spoken fallback below the first price exists only after five offers on paper and is never written down.
