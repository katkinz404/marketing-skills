# Audit Rubric and Dollar Ranking

The full external rubric behind the Quote Check, the eight dimensions it covers, the six rows that are free, the method that turns scores into a one-page dollar ranking, and the ethics rules. Internal reference; the word "audit" never reaches a buyer. Every source was retrieved by web search on 2026-09-15; "not read at source" means the page was not opened from this environment.

## The pocket-read six (free, 45 minutes)

Rows 3 and 4 combined (rating and count with the date), row 6 (reply rate), row 11 (quote request path), row 8 (name and phone match), row 18 (last post), and row 14 (the sheet, only if he hands one over). Everything else waits for the paid page, because a full local audit runs about four hours by published guides (Semrush and StoreRocket audit guides, vendor, not read at source) and four hours of free work per prospect is the worst economics in the funnel.

## Dollar ranking: from scores to one page

1. Take the five rows with the largest plausible dollar effect for this owner. His own numbers (intake and log) outrank every vendor figure.
2. Order by dollars, not by dimension. Free fixes first. Ties to the cheaper fix. Rows with no dollar figure go last.
3. Each row on the page carries: what is true today (FACT, dated); compared with (whose standard); what it may be costing (YOU SAID, HYPOTHESIS, or UNKNOWN); one next step; cost to fix; who, by when.
4. Formulas live on the back: missed calls x close rate x average job; quotes per month x (close rate with follow-up minus today's) x average job; dollars waiting x days waiting; referral share x jobs x average job.
5. Format rules from audit-reporting guidance (Ramp and others, https://ramp.com/blog/audit-report-template, trade press, not read at source): one page, verdict first, findings in dollars, no judgmental words, a named next step per row.
6. Vendor figures appear only as "one national vendor's figure."

## Judgment ranking for a solo exterior trade

For a solo painter at a typical job around $4,000 to $5,000 (judgment): the final-check leak and silent quotes first (rows 14 and 16, from what he says), then review response and rating (rows 6 and 4), then the quote document itself, then profile completeness, then name and phone consistency and social activity last.

## Review arithmetic

A profile with 4 reviews at 3.0 has 12 stars. (12 + 5n) divided by (4 + n) reaches 4.5 at n = 12. Three or four new reviews do not get there (n = 4 gives 4.0). About two in three people asked leave a review (67%, BrightLocal LCRS 2026, not read at source), so plan about 18 identical asks.

## Ethics rules

- Contact the business only under your own name, for a real reason. No personas, no fake quote requests, no asking a friend to call.
- Rows 12 and 13 (timed enquiry and call answer rate) are not used in this skill. A voicemail from a consultant "checking response time" is a cold call he deletes, and the number is wrong before you have met (panel finding). Score the phone from what he tells you.
- No recording. Georgia prospects sit across a one-party-consent line.
- Nothing from a document he did not hand over. A customer's copy, a relative's copy, a counter clerk's copy: out.
- Review work follows the FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR Part 465, effective 2024-10-21, https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers, government) and Google's content policy (https://support.google.com/business/answer/7400114, vendor): no incentives tied to sentiment, no gating, one identical ask to everyone. Neither is recited to a buyer.
