# Funnel and Channel Canon

Every figure was retrieved by web search on 2026-09-15 unless dated otherwise; "not read at source" means the page itself was not opened from this environment. ## How the funnel uses this

1. Five connectors first, because referrals plus direct contact are the channel and the consultant has no clients to refer her yet.
2. Five planned touches, each useful, because the meeting comes late and the buyer ends it when he wants.
3. A day-2 call after every offer, because proposals that go quiet past two days are usually lost.
4. One screen on the web, no form, because a page without traffic converts nobody and a tradesman taps to call.
5. The free thing is a card, the written page is paid, because free written work is the worst economics in the set and a paid read converts better.
6. No cold texts, no cold email without an address, no recording, no personas, no growth claims.
