## Statistics to refuse

Never repeat these as fact, even with a caveat. Each line names the figure and the reason it is refused.

- "SEO closes at 14.6% vs 1.7% for paid" (vendor-recycled; original source untraceable)
- "21x more likely to qualify within 5 minutes" (vendor-recycled; a 2007 six-firm B2B vendor study)
- "78% of customers buy from the first responder" (unattributed)
- "62% of home-service calls go unanswered" (unattributed; vendor-recycled)
- "391% lift at 1-minute response" (vendor-recycled)
- Referral customers "16-25% higher LTV / 18-37% lower churn / refer at 2-3x" (vendor-recycled; misread from unrelated studies)
- "Rule of 7" (unattributed marketing folklore)
- "42% of GBP suspensions are address failures" (unattributed)
- "NAP errors suppress 40% of rankings" (unattributed; vendor-recycled)
- "12% LSA fee" (misread; Local Services Ads bill per lead, not a percentage)
- "30-50% interview no-show" (unattributed; vendor-recycled)
- Form-field drop-off percentages (vendor-recycled; not transferable across forms)
- First-clean-to-recurring benchmarks, published range 3.7% to 60% (unreliable; measure as counts in your own data)
